01 / Research

Across disciplines.

My interests span economics and anthropology, with a focus on transdisciplinary and heterodox approaches to social research. My projects examine subsistence organization and agricultural commercialization in the United States, including their connections to food assistance, as well as the development of zero-waste city research through bibliometric analysis.

  • Structural transformation
  • Subsistence organization
  • Agricultural commercialization
  • Urban sustainability
  • Bibliometrics
